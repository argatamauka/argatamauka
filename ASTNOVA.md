# ASTnova — Official Brand Identity

**Official wordmark:** ASTnova  
**Pronunciation:** AST Nova  
**Tagline:** IGNITE WHAT'S POSSIBLE.  
**Brand pillars:** Technology · Creativity · Exploration

## Meaning

**AST** comes from the initials of Arga Setia Tamauka. **Nova** is inspired by the astronomical phenomenon in which a star suddenly increases in brightness.

Together, **ASTnova** represents a personal identity that brings new light through technology, creativity, and exploration. Every idea carries the potential to grow into something useful, original, and meaningful.

## Brand philosophy

> Every great innovation begins with curiosity. Every possibility begins with an idea. ASTnova exists to turn those ideas into something extraordinary.

ASTnova stands for purposeful curiosity, continuous learning, thoughtful experimentation, and the courage to turn possibilities into real work.

## Three pillars

- **Technology:** use engineering and digital tools to solve meaningful problems.
- **Creativity:** transform original ideas into clear, useful, and memorable experiences.
- **Exploration:** keep learning, experimenting, and discovering new possibilities.

## Brand personality

Curious, innovative, visionary, independent, and exploratory.

## Visual direction

- Midnight Space — `#101522`
- Cosmic Indigo — `#27365C`
- Nova Blue — `#8DA8FF`
- Stellar White — `#F3F5FC`

The visual character is futuristic, mysterious, and elegant. Product interfaces may keep their own established design systems; ASTnova should appear as a consistent personal-brand signature rather than forcing every product into the same UI theme.

## Usage rules

1. Write the wordmark exactly as **ASTnova**: uppercase `AST`, lowercase `nova`.
2. Keep each product's own name. Attribute independent projects with **A project by ASTnova** or **Part of ASTnova**.
3. Use the tagline **IGNITE WHAT'S POSSIBLE.** consistently.
4. Apply the identity to personal portfolio work, independent software, experiments, and creative projects. Do not overwrite school/coursework identities or third-party brands.
5. Let the work demonstrate the brand: useful technology, thoughtful creativity, and continuous exploration.
