# ASTvibe — Official Brand Identity

**Official wordmark:** ASTvibe  
**Pronunciation:** AST vibe  
**Tagline:** CREATE YOUR OWN VIBE.  
**Brand pillars:** Technology · Creativity · Innovation · Exploration

## Meaning

**AST** comes from the initials of Arga Setia Tamauka. **Vibe** represents character, energy, atmosphere, and creative style.

Together, **ASTvibe** represents a personal creative-technology identity: a recognizable character behind the ideas, software, and digital experiences being created.

## Brand philosophy

> Build with curiosity. Create with character. Keep exploring.

ASTvibe stands for turning curiosity into practical work, approaching technology creatively, and continuing to experiment and improve.

## Brand pillars

- **Technology:** build useful digital products and solve real problems.
- **Creativity:** give ideas a clear, thoughtful, and memorable form.
- **Innovation:** experiment, improve, and find better approaches.
- **Exploration:** keep learning and discovering new possibilities.

## Brand personality

Modern, curious, independent, expressive, thoughtful, and forward-looking.

## Naming system

Every independent product and project name starts with the exact prefix **ASTvibe**.

Examples:
- **ASTvibe Portfolio**
- **ASTvibe Student Planner**
- **ASTvibe Student Planner V1**
- **ASTvibe Student Planner V2**
- **ASTvibe Study AI**
- **ASTvibe [Future Product]**

Keep each product's own interface, product-specific visual style, and functionality. The ASTvibe prefix is the common identity; it does not require every product to use the same UI theme.

## Writing rules

1. Write the official wordmark exactly as **ASTvibe**: uppercase AST, lowercase vibe.
2. Use the tagline **CREATE YOUR OWN VIBE.**
3. Start product/project titles with **ASTvibe**.
4. Do not replace school/coursework titles, third-party brands, or lecturer-provided source materials merely to add branding.
5. Use consistent ASTvibe attribution in portfolio and project documentation.
