# ASThorizon Brand Identity

**Official wordmark:** ASThorizon  
**Tagline:** BEYOND THE KNOWN.  
**Pillars:** Technology · Creativity · Exploration

## Meaning

**AST** comes from the initials of Arga Setia Tamauka. **Horizon** represents the farthest visible boundary: a reminder that knowledge, imagination, and possibility can always extend further.

Together, **ASThorizon** is Arga's personal identity for exploring technology, developing creative ideas, and turning possibilities into useful work.

## Brand philosophy

> Explore what is unknown. Imagine what is possible. Create what comes next.

ASThorizon stands for curiosity with purpose, continuous learning, thoughtful experimentation, and the courage to build beyond current limits.

## Three pillars

- **Technology:** use engineering and digital tools to solve meaningful problems.
- **Creativity:** turn original ideas into clear, useful, and memorable experiences.
- **Exploration:** keep learning, experimenting, and discovering new possibilities.

## Visual direction

- Midnight Navy — `#10141D`
- Silver White — `#E8EAF0`
- Cosmic Blue — `#8D9FCB`
- Nebula Purple — `#A995D1`

The visual character is futuristic, mysterious, and elegant. Product interfaces may keep their own established visual systems; ASThorizon should appear as a consistent parent-brand signature rather than forcing every product into the same UI theme.

## Usage rules

1. Write the wordmark exactly as **ASThorizon**: uppercase `AST`, lowercase `horizon`.
2. Keep each product's own name. Attribute independent projects with **A project by ASThorizon** or **Part of ASThorizon**.
3. Use the tagline **BEYOND THE KNOWN.** consistently.
4. Apply the identity to personal portfolios, independent software, experiments, and creative work. Do not overwrite school/coursework identities or third-party brands.
5. Let the work demonstrate the brand: useful technology, thoughtful creativity, and continuous exploration.
